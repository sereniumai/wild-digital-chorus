# Chorus — brand & voice portal (demo)

One portal for a company and its people. It learns the company's brand and voice from its website and LinkedIn, learns each employee's voice from their LinkedIn, and then:

- **Company:** turns one line of news (or a pasted article / link) into LinkedIn, Instagram and X posts in the company voice, with an on-brand image in every platform size.
- **Each person:** a profile photo cut out on a brand colour, the company banner, a LinkedIn headline and About, and an "I've joined" post — in their own voice. They can also post about any company news as themselves, with three different angles.

Demo workspace: Unifyr. Built by Wild Digital. Single static page, no build step.

**Notes:** in this demo the writing and images are pre-generated from Unifyr's public site and news; live writing switches on with an Anthropic API key (click the "Demo" pill). Photo cut-out runs in the browser (MediaPipe). Nothing you type or upload leaves your browser.
